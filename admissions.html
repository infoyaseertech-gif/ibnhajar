Admission process

★ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common admissions questions

What age can a child be admitted?

Admission is open to children ready for Primary 1 through JSS 3. Contact the admissions office if you're unsure which level fits your child.

Is boarding compulsory?

Yes — Ibn Hajar Foundation-Zaria runs as a full boarding programme so students can follow the structured Qur'an memorization schedule alongside academics.

Can a student join mid-session?

Mid-session admission is considered case by case, depending on space and the student's readiness to join an ongoing memorization schedule.

How will I know if my application was successful?

Our admissions office reviews every application and will reach out directly by phone or email using the contact details you provide on the form.

Can I pay fees in instalments?

Speak with the Bursary office after admission is confirmed — payment arrangements can be discussed directly with them.
