★ WHY IBN HAJAR

A boarding programme built on four pillars

Qur'an Memorization

An intensive boarding-camp programme structured to guide every student to complete the memorization of the entire Qur'an within three years.

Tajweed & Islamic Studies

Proper articulation and recitation of the Qur'an taught alongside Aqeedah, Fiqh and Seerah, grounding students in sound Islamic understanding.

Conventional Academics

A full Primary and Junior Secondary curriculum runs alongside Qur'anic studies, so students graduate prepared for both deen and the world.

Qualified, Caring Teachers

An experienced faculty combines academic qualification with genuine pastoral care for every boarding student's growth and discipline.

★ ABOUT THE FOUNDATION

More than a school — a home for character

Located at No. 52 Unguwar Katuka, Zaria City, Ibn Hajar Foundation-Zaria brings together Qur'an memorization, Islamic values, academic studies, discipline and moral development inside one boarding community. Our philosophy is simple and demanding at once: a person must first raise themselves — in character, discipline and knowledge — before they can build anything good for humankind.
