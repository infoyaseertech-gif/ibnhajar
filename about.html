About Ibn Hajar Foundation-Zaria

Our history, our philosophy, and the boarding programme that shapes it.

★ OUR STORY

Founded to raise a generation grounded in the Qur'an

Ibn Hajar Foundation-Zaria was established in Zaria City as an Islamic boarding school for Qur'an memorization, combined with a full Primary and Junior Secondary academic curriculum. The Foundation was built on the conviction that a child's character and knowledge must be raised together — not one at the expense of the other.

Today, the Foundation houses a boarding community where students live, study, and memorize the Qur'an under the guidance of qualified and caring teachers, within a structured, disciplined and morally grounded environment.

★ VISION & MISSION

Our guiding purpose

Vision

To raise a generation of Huffaz (memorizers of the Qur'an) who are also academically accomplished, morally upright, and equipped to serve their families, communities and humankind at large.

Mission

To provide a disciplined boarding environment that combines intensive Qur'an memorization, Tajweed and Islamic studies with conventional academic education, delivered by qualified and caring teachers who guide every student's moral and academic development.

★ LIFE ON CAMPUS

What our boarding programme provides

  • Full-time boarding accommodation and supervised daily routine
  • Structured Qur'an memorization schedule with revision (murajaah)
  • Daily Islamic studies, Tajweed and conventional class periods
  • Feeding, hygiene support and hostel welfare for all boarders
  • Discipline and moral development guided by qualified staff
